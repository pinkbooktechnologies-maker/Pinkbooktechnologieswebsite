01 / Our story

The best solution begins with a better question.

PinkBook was built around a simple way of working: get close to the problem, understand the people it affects and make software that earns its place in their day.

What we believe

Technology is most powerful when the complexity stays behind the scenes.

PinkBook / Product philosophy

Our work moves between healthcare, education, commerce and connected products. The domains change; the discipline stays the same. We turn complicated workflows into experiences people can understand and systems teams can depend on.

We stay involved from discovery and interface design through engineering, release and the next useful improvement.
