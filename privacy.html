1. About this notice

This notice explains how PinkBook Technologies handles information submitted through this website and its contact channels. It applies to project enquiries, general questions and career communications.

2. Information we receive

The enquiry form may collect your name, work email, company, service interest and project message. When you submit the form, those details are sent securely to PinkBook Technologies’ enquiry service and the configured administrative mailbox.

If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp, or send a job application, we receive the information you choose to share through that channel. Please do not include passwords, patient records, identity documents or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

3. How we use information

We use enquiry information to understand your request, reply to you, prepare relevant project discussions and maintain appropriate business correspondence. Career information is used to assess and discuss the advertised role.

4. External services

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. The optional contact-page Google map loads only after you select “Load Google map”. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details under their own policies.

Email, WhatsApp, social links and external map links open third-party services with their own privacy practices. See Google’s privacy policy and WhatsApp’s privacy policy.

5. Storage & retention

This website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may retain normal security and access logs. Enquiries and business correspondence are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential or active business relationship, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

6. Your questions & requests

To ask about information you have shared, or request access, correction or deletion where applicable, email hello@pinkbooktechnologies.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

7. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our website, service providers or information-handling practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
